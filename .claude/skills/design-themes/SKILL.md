# Designs

Paths in this skill are relative to `packages/map/`. Run npm scripts from the repository root.

Each design has two halves that must stay in step:

1. **Canvas** (`src/themes.ts`): a `Theme` object with ocean/land colours, land texture (`halftone`, `matrix`, `none`), number of engraved water lines, graticule, rivers, relief ink, dot colours, the flat projection, and `decor` (drawings under the dots from `src/map/decor.ts`, or `null`). The globe always uses the orthographic projection.
2. **Chrome** (`src/style.css`): a `:root[data-theme="<id>"]` block of tokens (`--page`, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--rule`, `--accent`, `--on-accent`, fonts), plus any theme-specific rules further down (search for `data-theme="<id>"`).

Every design shows the same name and tagline from `src/brand.ts`. The row above it and the typography differ per design in `renderMasthead()` in `src/main.ts`. Don't give a design its own publication name.

## Adding a design

1. Add the id to `ThemeId` and an entry in `THEMES`.
2. Add a token block in `style.css`. Pick fonts from `@fontsource` (`npm i @fontsource/<font>`) and import the weights in `src/main.ts`. Don't load fonts from a third-party CDN.
3. Style `.telegram-word` and `.telegram-big` for the design with `font-family`, `font-weight`, `letter-spacing` and colour only, never the `font` shorthand, so the length-based size (`--len`) still applies. Check that "Encouragement" fits a 375-pixel phone.
4. Pictures that say what a design is (a frog on a pad, a moon with a bear) go in `src/map/scenery.ts` as small SVG pictures with a spot and an open-water radius `r`; add the list to `test/scenery.test.ts`. Line drawings go in `src/map/decor.ts`, called once from `render()` in `view.ts` under the dots. Use fixed lon/lat spots (or areas, like Pirate's `WAVE_FIELDS`) in open ocean and add every list to `test/decor.test.ts`, which checks they are clear of land and far from every outlet's city. No text, and no small filled circles that could read as a dot; a large drawing may be filled with the paper colour so water lines don't show through it. Size a drawing from the open sea around its spot (Pirate's `r`), so it never reaches land at any zoom. Build shapes once as `Path2D` and draw them with a transform; the canvas redraws every frame while dragging. Backgrounds outside the globe can be CSS on `.map` (see the Space starfield).
5. Add the id to `themes` in `scripts/screenshots.ts`, and make its preview for the Design picker: `npm run map:build && npm run map:thumbs <id>` writes `public/thumbs/<id>.jpg` (decision 132; `test/designs.test.ts` fails without it). Make it again whenever the design's look changes.
6. Check the look in both views and at phone width with the `run-app` skill's screenshot flow.

## Rules that don't change between designs
- No text on the map canvas, no borders (see packages/map/AGENTS.md "Neutrality rules").
- "Fresh" must stay distinguishable from ordinary dots: by colour, or, for monochrome designs, by a dotted outer ring (set `fresh` equal to `dot` and `drawDots` adds it). The solid outer ring means importance 4 or 5 in every design (decision 57); keep the three symbols readable.
- Pixel designs set `pixel` (the canvas renders at 1/pixel resolution). `dotShape` is one of the outlines in `src/map/marks.ts` (circle, square, diamond, bevel, button, hex, pad, star4, star5, star6, flower, gumdrop, shield, block, shell, squircle, house, loop, x, pin, ticket, stub, teacup, nugget, cube); shape never carries meaning. The map and the Key both draw from `markPath` (the outer ring from `markRing`, a circle round Pirate's X), so a new shape goes there and nowhere else, and hollow, filled and ringed must all still read in it.
- Check the phone toolbar stays one row at 360 pixels wide with the design's fonts.
- No inline style attributes (`style="..."` in markup or through `h()`): the live site's CSP (`style-src 'self'`) blocks them, though the dev server sends no CSP, so they work locally and break in production. Use a class in `style.css`, or set `el.style.x` from code, which the CSP allows.
- Never cut text off. The toolbar's dropdowns keep the browser's width, with a minimum the page measures from their chosen text in the design's own font, letter spacing, case and small capitals (`fitSelects` in `main.ts`), so don't give a `.pick select` a `max-width` or `text-overflow: ellipsis`; Safari sizes a select from the bare text and would drop the last letter. Nothing else that holds words may clip them either: no fixed widths with `overflow: hidden` on buttons, headings or labels.
- `tilt` (a tilted camera in Map view) and `lowPoly` (triangle terrain) are Polygon Kingdom's. Under a tilt, places are drawn through `placeAt()` in `view.ts` so markers, arcs and tuning follow the camera; draw thousands of shapes as SVG path text in one `Path2D`, not as separate calls.
- Any design may take `tilt`: `tiltEye` sets the perspective, `tiltFar` the draw distance and `tiltMinZoom` how far out Map view may zoom (Night Drive). A design that draws the world a way of its own sets `surface` and gets a frame from `view.ts` (`src/map/surface.ts`; Night Drive, Cross Stitch and Rose Window in `neon.ts`, `stitch.ts`, `glass.ts`; Spreadsheet, Market Terminal, Country Club and Sleeper Car in `sheet.ts`, `terminal.ts`, `club.ts`, `rail.ts`). Chrome a design adds around the map (a formula bar, cell headers, a coordinate readout, a crest, a clock) goes in `src/ui/extras.ts`, hidden by CSS in every other design; text there goes in as text, never as HTML. Couch Potato's home screen of channel tiles is its own module, `src/ui/channels.ts` (decision 100): a modal dialog only that design opens, whose tiles call back into `main.ts` (`openChannel`), and its headline card over the map is `renderCard` in `main.ts`, the one overlay rule 2 allows: one place's headlines from the panel, one at a time, with no place name. Keep static parts (skies, hoops, tracery) in an offscreen canvas keyed by frame size and globe radius, and measure a frame while dragging against Polygon Kingdom's.
- `tilt` (a tilted camera in Map view) is Polygon Kingdom's and Nightclub's; `lowPoly` (triangle terrain) is Polygon Kingdom's. Under a tilt, places are drawn through `placeAt()` in `view.ts` so markers, arcs and tuning follow the camera; draw thousands of shapes as SVG path text in one `Path2D`, not as separate calls.
- A camera that bends the whole picture is a `warp` (`src/map/warp.ts`, decision 75): a picture tube's barrel curve (Arcade Cabinet, Map view only), a big screen turned in perspective (Stadium Jumbotron), a map lying on a desk under a canted camera (Film Noir). The view sends land, arcs and places through it and takes drags back through it, and the frame's centre never moves. A new warp needs `fwd`, `inv` and `unpan`, and a case in `test/warp.test.ts`. A design that frames the map (Radar Sweep's scope, the tube, the big screen) returns `inside` and `clip` from its draw function, so places outside the frame are neither drawn nor tuned; `under` draws beneath the markers (the radar's halos) and `over` above them (scanlines, the LED mesh, the crowd).
- Motion on the canvas: set `motion` in the theme and the view redraws at most 20 times a second, never in a hidden tab or with reduced motion (the frame's `still` is then true: draw no motion). Keep the map under the moving parts in `cachedPicture` (`surface.ts`) so a still view costs one image. Never move, resize or dim a marker; a halo or glow is extra.
- A surface's draw function may return the milliseconds until it wants its next frame (Toy Train Set's trains, Sketchbook's line boil, Chalkboard's smudge, decision 76). The frame carries `time` (held at 0 with `still` set for reduced motion), the drag's `trail` and the `anchors` (every place shown). The view never asks for a frame while the tab is hidden or for reduced motion. Keep the static part in `stillLayer()` from `src/map/handmade.ts`, so a moving frame only copies it. Things at sea go in a spot list with a radius and into `test/handmade.test.ts`; wide soft strokes use `wideCoast()`. `tiltOut` makes the tilt follow the zoom (Pop-up Book).
- Keep text contrast readable: body text at least 4.5:1 against `--panel`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for anything animated.
- **No flashing** (WCAG 2.3.1). Nothing may flash more than three times a second or change brightness sharply over a large area: no strobe, no neon flicker, no light that swings 10% in brightness within a third of a second. Colour shifts take seconds. `test/scenes.test.ts` checks this for Nightclub's floor colours and the mirror ball's glints; add a check like it for any new moving light.
- `scene` (Nightclub `club`, Poolside `pool`, Snow Globe `snow`, decision 71) gives a design its own camera and moving light (`src/map/scenes.ts`, `renderScene` in `view.ts`). The map is drawn once per view into an off-screen canvas and only the light is repainted, twelve frames a second, none while the tab is hidden, none with reduced motion. Places go through the same camera as the land (`scenePlace`), so a new camera needs its warp there and, if it enlarges the centre, in `sceneMag` for dragging. Only light may fall on the map; rooms, snow, frost, glass, spotlights and lasers stay outside it. `globeScale` draws the globe smaller to leave room around it.
- Motion on the canvas (Aquarium's fish and water, Lava Lamp's wax; `src/map/ambient.ts`, decision 77): give the `surface` a rate in `AMBIENT_MS` and the view asks for frames at it, never for reduced motion. Read time from `motionTime()`, which holds still for reduced motion. Draw what doesn't move through a `StillLayer` (drawn straight while the view moves, cached once it stops) and copy frame-sized static layers with `drawPart`, so a frame of motion stays cheap. Nothing may flash more than 3 times a second or change brightness sharply over a large area, and markers never move.
- The Design menu is grouped (`DESIGN_GROUPS` in `src/themes.ts`): add a new id to the group it belongs in. An id in no group still shows, under Other.
- Performance: the canvas redraws every frame while dragging, using the 110m basemap. Anything expensive goes behind `if (!this.interacting)`.
